Course Overview

This graduate-level course provides a comprehensive introduction to deep generative models, covering both theoretical foundations and practical applications. The course explores state-of-the-art generative models including Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Normalizing Flows, Diffusion Models, and more.

Course Information

  • Instructor: Dr. Sajjad Amini
  • Semester: Fall 2024
  • Credits: 3
  • Prerequisites: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Probability and Statistics
  • Course Website: generative-ai-sut.github.io

Course Objectives

  • Understand the theoretical foundations of deep generative models
  • Study various architectures and their mathematical formulations
  • Gain hands-on experience with implementing and training generative models
  • Explore cutting-edge research and applications in the field
  • Develop critical thinking for evaluating and improving generative models

Topics Covered

  1. Introduction to Generative Modeling
  2. Autoregressive Models
  3. Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)
  4. Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)
  5. Normalizing Flows
  6. Diffusion Models
  7. Energy-Based Models
  8. Advanced Topics (e.g., Transformers for Generation, Multimodal Generation)
  9. Applications in Computer Vision, NLP, and Beyond

Course Format

  • Programming Assignments
